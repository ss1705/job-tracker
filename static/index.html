Held-out test
Held-out emails are ones the parser was never tuned on. Once frozen, don't change parser.py or gmail.py; errors found here are future work, not fixes to re-score. Protocol: docs/EVAL_PROTOCOL.md.
Evaluation set

Only people you reached out to are imported; incoming requests and conversations others started are skipped.
